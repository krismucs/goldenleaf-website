Contact

Email mousam@goldenleaf-ai.com with your store's .myshopify.com domain, the page you were on, and a screenshot if something looked wrong. Billing questions are welcome too: your subscription and the free trial are managed on the Plans page inside the app, and cancelling there or uninstalling stops future charges.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store and approve the permissions it asks for.
  2. On the Plans page, choose the marketing plan, the inventory plan or both, and approve the subscription in Shopify. Nothing is charged for the first 30 days.
  3. On the Data page, run the sync so the app has your products, inventory and orders.
  4. On the Ad spend page, upload a Meta Ads Manager or Google Ads CSV export if you use the marketing plan.
  5. Check the Settings page: product costs, supplier lead times and the value of leftover stock shape the inventory plan.
  6. The Weekly plan page shows the Monday marketing plan and the inventory plan with purchase orders you can download as CSV. The Inventory outlook page shows the forecast for each product.

Your data

The app reads store data only for the plans it produces and deletes everything after you uninstall, as described in the privacy policy. To have your data removed sooner, email us from the address on your Shopify account.
