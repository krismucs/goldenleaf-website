OptimizedMarketingInventory

Plan reorders and ad budgets together. Built on your sales history, stock and margins.

OptimizedMarketingInventory turns your sales history, stock levels, product costs and ad spend into two plans you review and approve. The inventory plan forecasts demand for every product with a range of outcomes, sizes purchase orders around your margins, supplier lead times and the value of leftover stock, and exports them as CSV for your suppliers. The marketing plan shows every Monday which products to push, hold or clear, with budget moves checked against stock and evidence.

What it does

  • Demand forecast per product with uncertainty ranges and weeks of cover
  • Purchase quantities sized by margins, lead times and leftover stock value
  • One purchase order per supplier, downloadable as CSV
  • Monday push, hold and clear list with a reason for every product
  • Meta and Google Ads spend imported from their CSV exports

Nothing runs without your approval: the app recommends, you decide what to order and what to spend.
